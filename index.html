        Swim Academy · Quick Guide

        How to Play & Scoring

        Glide past reef rocks, munch golden pellets and survive with fish-count math!

        ❤️ 3 Hearts🌊 10 Levels
        ↑↓
        01 · Motion

        Move Your Fish

        Use arrow keys or W / S. On a phone or with a mouse, press and drag on the water - the fish follows your finger.

        02 · Evasion

        Dodge the Rocks

        Swim through the gaps. Each level is faster and the gaps get smaller - and from level 4 they drift. Each column earns +25 pts.

        03 · Feed

        Collect Fish Food

        Glowing pellets earn +50 pts each. Swim close and they drift into your mouth.

        04 · Health

        3 Lives & Checkpoints

        A rock hit costs 1 life and glides you back to your last checkpoint.

        05 · Instant revive

        Fish Count Challenge

        Lose all 3 lives? Count the fish correctly to earn +50 pts and restart with full lives!

        ⭐ Scoring Guide
        🪨 Rock column passed+25 pts
        🟡 Fish food collected+50 pts
        🐠 Correct fish count+50 pts
        ✅ Level completed+250 pts
        💡
        Tip: keep your fish near the centre of the screen - it makes dodging high and low rocks much easier.
